Clear answers

Oopbuy spreadsheet FAQ.

Understand what this guide offers and how to use a product shortlist thoughtfully.

What is an oopbuy spreadsheet?

An oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-organizing list used to collect shopping links and compare details such as category, size, listed price, and inspection notes. Individual lists differ in coverage, accuracy, and maintenance.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, and it does not represent Oopbuy or the fashion brands mentioned.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

This page provides category links and a shopping checklist. It does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or a live inventory feed.

What should I check before saving a product?

Record the product link, selected variation, measurements, listed price, date checked, and any unanswered questions. Verify current details on the destination listing before making a purchase.

Does a product photo confirm authenticity?

No. A photograph, brand name, or quality-control image alone does not establish authenticity. Check the seller’s evidence and applicable brand verification options.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s measurements with a garment you already own. Check the measurement method, units, and variation-specific size chart instead of relying only on a size label.
